November 2026 passenger-interface research prototype

Scene images: AI-generated, project-owned concept assets approved for this prototype layout. Intro day/night images share the intended car and street composition; photographic identity and non-weather differences need researcher review. Dynamic window overlays are local procedural effects, not recorded driving footage.

Maps: Geographic street/building geometry from the public vis.gl deck.gl-data trips example (https://github.com/visgl/deck.gl-data/tree/master/examples/trips). Derived map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under ODbL: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Route computed locally for illustration; no location labels, live location, remote tiles or external routing calls. Day/night maps share geometry. Visible attribution is retained on the participant screen.

Narration: locally generated with Kokoro-82M v1.0 (Apache-2.0 model; https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), kokoro-onnx (MIT; https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx). Voice af_heart, US English. Four fixed PCM recordings; the same recording is used for both environment conditions. Research-team listening review is required before use for data collection. Model and generation dependencies are not distributed with the site.

Pin icon geometry follows the Lucide map-pin (ISC): Copyright (c) for portions of Lucide are held by Cole Bemis 2013-2022 as part of Feather (MIT). All other copyright (c) for Lucide are held by Lucide Contributors 2022.
